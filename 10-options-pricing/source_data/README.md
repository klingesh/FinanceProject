# source_data: original downloads behind every number

The six NSE option-chain files and the two Yahoo Finance files were downloaded on 31-Oct-2026 into `data/raw/`. The copies here are identical (same SHA-256 hashes, listed in `download_log.csv`). Everything else was saved on 01-Nov-2026 by `src/fetch_source_data.py`, which also writes the two Excel copies and checks that the number or phrase used in this project appears in each saved file. The NIFTY 50 factsheet, the RBI page and the Economic Times T-bill article are copies of the files saved for Project 8 on the same day. The analysis (`src/run_all.py` and the notebook) reads only `data/raw/`, never this folder.

Units: option prices and index levels in index points (1 point = ₹1 per unit; 1 NIFTY lot = 65 units); bid and ask quantities in units; open interest and volume as reported by NSE (contracts); NSE's implied volatility in % a year; India VIX in % a year.

**News articles are not in the GitHub copy.** The six saved pages from news sites and broker websites in `news_and_macro/` are copyrighted, so they stay on the author's PC and are left out of the public repository (see `.gitignore`). Each one is listed below with its full link and the fact taken from it. The RBI page in the same folder, the NSE data, pages, circulars and FAQ, the NSE Indices factsheet and the Yahoo Finance data are included.
