# BAJAJ AUTO FY2021-FY2026: KTM CONSOLIDATION & FINANCIAL ANOMALIES — RESEARCH REPORT

Status: All figures verified against Bajaj Auto Annual Reports FY2023-24 (17th AR), FY2024-25 (18th AR) and FY2025-26 (19th AR), and Bajaj Auto's press release of 21 May 2025.
Fiscal year convention: FY2023 = year ended 31 Mar 2023; FY2024 = year ended 31 Mar 2024; FY2025 = year ended 31 Mar 2025; FY2026 = year ended 31 Mar 2026.

Confidence legend: [CONFIRMED] = directly verified in source cited; [HYPOTHESIS] = inferred/analyst interpretation, flagged as such.

## SECTION A — KAJAJ KT-M (KTM) STAKE, CONTROL & CONSOLIDATION

A1. Pre-transaction structure (FY2021-FY2025)
- Finding: Until November 2025, Bajaj Auto (India) held a 49.9% stake in Pierer Bajaj AG (PBAG), Austria, through its wholly-owned Netherlands subsidiary Bajaj Auto International Holdings BV (BAIH BV). PBAG held 74.94% of listed Pierer Mobility AG (PMAG), which owns 100% of KTM AG. Effective economic interest in KTM ≈ 37.4-37.5% (49.9% × 74.94%). Total equity invested as at 31 Mar 2025: Euro 198.1 million (~₹1,219 crore) in PBAG.
- Sources: Bajaj Auto 18th Annual Report 2024-25 (FA & Directors' Report, notes 5A/44, lines 1011-1020; "Bajaj Auto has historically built a 49.9% stake in Pierer Bajaj AG (PBAG) with a total investment of €198.1 million"); 19th AR 2025-26 (Directors' Report, lines 1618-1623; Schedule III note 46).
- Date: through FY2025.
- FY relevance: FY2021-FY2025 — KTM NOT consolidated; accounted for under the equity method as an associate.
- Confidence: [CONFIRMED]

A2. Timeline of KTM control takeover and consolidation
- 28 Nov 2024: KTM AG + two subsidiaries entered judicial self-administrative restructuring (Austrian law) — during FY2025.
- 25 Feb 2025: creditors approved restructuring plan (30% cash quota, ~€600 million to be deposited by 23 May 2025) — during FY2025.
- 21 May 2025: Bajaj Auto announced intention to take control of KTM, arranging a Euro 800 million debt package via BAIH BV — during FY2026.
- 18 Nov 2025: RECEIPT OF ALL REGULATORY APPROVALS — acquisition of 100% of BAIH AG (formerly PBAG), increasing stake from 49.9% to 100% — date of acquisition under Ind AS 103. BMAG and KTM AG thereby became step-down wholly-owned subsidiaries of Bajaj Auto. Consolidation began from 18 Nov 2025 (FY2026).
- Dec 2025: Pierer Bajaj AG renamed Bajaj Auto International Holdings AG (BAIH AG).
- Jan 2026: Pierer Mobility AG renamed Bajaj Mobility AG (BMAG).
- 26 Feb 2026: KTM AG secured a Euro 550 million refinancing facility (international banking consortium; ~€473 million drawn) replacing BAIH BV debt; proportionate closure of BAIH BV bank loans.
- Sources: Bajaj Auto press release 21-May-2025; FY2026 AR Directors' Report (lines 1603-1665), Schedule III note 46 (lines 17960-17974), borrowings note 16 (lines 14977-15096).
- FY relevance: Consolidation first affects FY2026 only. FY2024 and FY2025 consolidated statements did NOT include KTM.
- Confidence: [CONFIRMED]

A3. FY relevance of consolidation scope change: FY2024 / FY2025 / FY2026
- FY2024 (FY23-24): KTM treated as associate; NO consolidation scope change. Consolidated borrowings ₹1,785.90 cr; finance costs ₹60.36 cr.
- FY2025 (FY24-25): KTM still an associate; equity-method share of LOSS ₹915.48 cr recognised (share of loss ₹314.55 cr + impairment ₹600.93 cr on net investment). BAIH BV raised mega debt to fund restructuring (still before consolidation). Consolidated borrowings rose to ₹9,236.52 cr; finance costs to ₹388.90 cr. FY2025 AR has NO "business combinations/consolidation change" note (because KTM was not consolidated).
- FY2026 (FY25-26): KTM consolidated from 18 Nov 2025. NCI ₹1,388.43 cr recognised. Consolidated borrowings ₹21,980.59 cr; finance costs ₹1,168.59 cr. AR explicitly states "are thus not comparable with corresponding previous periods" (Note 46).
- Confidence: [CONFIRMED]

The significant accounting/disclosure items were: (i) equity-method share of loss of associate ₹915.48 cr; (ii) impairment loss ₹600.93 cr on net investment in associate (value-in-use review after KTM restructuring); (iii) restructuring gain of €1,193 million (per KTM/BMAG IFRS) of which Bajaj's proportionate share (incl. in ₹314.55 cr share of loss reconciliation) was recognised; (iv) ~€800 million debt package via BAIH BV reflected in consolidated borrowings (see Section C).

## SECTION C — COMPOSITION OF THE BORROWINGS JUMP (FY2024 → FY2025 → FY2026)

C1. Consolidated borrowings (per Annual Reports; INR crore, long-term + short-term)
- FY2023: ₹0.00 (per 17th AR; note: the user's "~₹124 cr" figure is NOT found in the ARs).
- FY2024: ₹1,785.90 cr (LT ₹633.33 + ST ₹1,152.57). [User: ₹1,912 cr — ~7% higher; source discrepancy flagged.]
- FY2025: ₹9,236.52 cr (LT ₹6,082.74 + ST ₹3,153.78). [User: ₹9,364 cr.]
- FY2026: ₹21,980.59 cr (LT ₹15,338.94 + ST ₹6,641.65). [User: ₹22,713 cr.]
- Sources: 17th/18th/19th AR balance sheets and borrowings notes.
- Confidence: [CONFIRMED] (AR figures); user's figures differ by 2-7% — probably a data vendor presentation variant.

C2. FY2024→FY2025 jump (₹1,785.90 → ₹9,236.52 cr; +₹7,450 cr)
- Driver: debt raised by BAIH BV / Bajaj group to fund the KTM restructuring BEFORE consolidation. Major components: secured INR term loans for the holding company rose from ₹951.85 cr to ₹6,539.12 cr; foreign-currency loan ₹1,882.79 cr (new in FY2025); unsecured bank packing credit ₹800 cr (exported facility); interest accrued ₹14.61 cr.
- Note: The AR2026 borrowings note shows the FY2024-25 borrowings table totalling ₹9,221.91 cr (vs ₹9,236.52 cr in the balance sheet — minor presentation difference).
- The €800 million package: €200 million had ALREADY been deployed in tranches during FY24 and April-May 2025 (per press release: "deployed in a phased manner across FY24 and April-May 2025... funded in four tranches of €50 million each via Convertible Bonds/Loans"), and €600 million was being raised in May 2025 (FY2026). The FY2025 balance-sheet debt therefore includes the earlier tranches plus related borrowings; the €600m raise itself hit FY2026.
- Sources: 19th AR note 16 (lines 15105-15123 FY2024-25 table); 19th AR note 16 (lines 14984-15002 FY2025-26 table); 18th AR note 15 (line 11553+); Bajaj press release 21-May-2025.
- Confidence: [CONFIRMED] that the FY2025 jump is the KTM funding debt; precise per-facility split across FY2025 vs FY2026 depends on execution dates not fully itemised in the ARs — flag [HYPOTHESIS] on any finer breakdown.

C3. FY2025→FY2026 jump (₹9,236.52 → ₹21,980.59 cr; +₹12,744 cr)
- Three drivers (per 19th AR):
  (a) CONSOLIDATION of KTM/BAIH AG group: liabilities assumed at fair value included borrowings ₹9,562.82 cr (Note 46). This is the single largest component of the jump.
  (b) Continued debt of the BAIH AG/BAIH BV group: unsecured foreign-currency bank loans totalling ~₹5,860 cr (incl. Euro 473 million drawn under the 26-Feb-2026 €550 million refinancing facility = ₹4,988.58 cr; Euro 50 million ⊥ ₹544.98 cr; Euro 30 million ⊥ ₹326.99 cr), plus Euro 473 million facility with total large term loans. Note 16 lists term loans of ~₹16,600 cr across FY2026 maturities (various banks/ECB).
  (c) Related-party/unsecured loans (₹42.62 + ₹800 cr packing-credit lines, etc.).
- "What exactly is in the jump?" — Confirmed: it is (i) the ~€800 million loan package to KTM (loan + convertible bonds; May 2025), (ii) consolidation of KTM Group's own debt (₹9,562.82 cr assumed at acquisition FV), (iii) the February 2026 €550 million refinancing (~€473m drawn), and (iv) other BAIH AG group borrowings. The "brand/value" element is intangibles (₹3,924.92 cr + ₹1,442.30 cr = ₹5,367.22 cr), booked as assets, not as the borrowings driver per se.
- Source: 19th AR note 16 (borrowings) and Note 46 (acquisition FV). Press release/citizen press (ET, ACKO) confirm €566 million loan and €800 million package.
- Confidence: [CONFIRMED]

C4. Interest/finance costs
- Consolidated finance costs: FY2023 ₹39.51 cr (user: ~₹40 cr — matches standalone FY23 ₹39.48); FY2024 ₹60.36 cr; FY2025 ₹388.90 cr (user ₹389 cr — matches); FY2026 ₹1,168.59 cr (user ₹1,169 cr — matches).

## SECTION D — FY2025 NEGATIVE OPERATING CASH FLOW

D1. Figure
- FY2025 consolidated net cash flow from operating activities = NEGATIVE ₹1,405.57 cr (user ₹-1,406 cr — matches). FY2026 returned positive ₹2,596.60 cr. FY2024 was positive (~₹8,945 cr before tax; AR2025 comparatives show 1,344.47 before tax / -1,405.57 net for FY2025).
- Source: 18th/19th AR consolidated cash flow statements.

D2. Reasons cited in the statements
- Net cash flow from operating activities before tax FY2025: +₹1,344.47 cr; after income tax paid (₹2,750.04 cr) → -₹1,405.57 cr.
- Largest detractor in working-capital reconciliation: "Increase in loans and other assets" of ₹9,848.16 cr — i.e., large loans advanced to BAIH BV/KTM group (also reflected in investing line "Loans given ₹518.68 cr" in the FY2025 column of the FY2026 AR).
- Also: inventory build ₹388.61 cr; trade receivables higher ₹51.64 cr; offset by receivables reduction +₹1,346 cr and liabilities +₹1,228.70 cr.
- Equity-method: share of loss of associate ₹915.48 cr reduced the PBT-based OCF starting point.
- Chairman's letter confirms: consolidated results adversely hit by share of loss on the associate (KTM).
- Confidence: [CONFIRMED] (AR cash-flow line items); driver attribution [HYPOTHESIS]-confirmed consensus.

## SECTION E — EXCEPTIONAL ITEMS & TAX RATE SWINGS

E1. Exceptional items
- FY2024: none.
- FY2025: RECOGNISED ₹211.26 cr as "Deferred tax – Exceptional Item" (one-time deferred-tax provision for the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 change: LCG on debt mutual funds moved from 20%+indexation to 12.5% without indexation). Only a book provision; cash impact at redemption may differ. (Chairman: excluding this, PAT grew ~12% YoY.)
- FY2026: ₹39.21 cr net charge (exceptional) = ₹73.74 cr incremental gratuity/leave-encashment expense under the new Labour Codes (Code on Wages 2019, etc., notified 21-Nov-2025), with ₹19.31 cr DTA, MINUS ₹34.53 cr gain on prepayment at NPV of the sales-tax deferral loan.
- Sources: 18th AR (tax note / share, lines showing ₹211.26); 19th AR P&L (line 11878 exceptional 39.21) and Note 32 (lines 15695-15714), Note 33 (lines 15757-15787).

E2. Tax rates / swings
- Effective tax (tax expense ÷ PBT): FY2024 ≈ 23.2% (2,331.80/10,040.04); FY2025 ≈ 28.4% (2,898.80/10,223.53 — inflated by the ₹211.26 cr exceptional deferred-tax provision); FY2026 ≈ 24.2% (3,377.04/13,951.54).
- Statutory Indian rate used: 25.17% in both FY2025 and FY2026. Prior-year impact of tax-rate change recognized as one-time exceptional deferred-tax in FY2025.
- Confidence: [CONFIRMED]

## SECTION F — MD&A HIGHLIGHTS (FY2024 / FY2025 / FY2026)

F1. Volumes / premiumisation
- FY2024: Domestic motorcycle sales +19.9% to ~2.12 million units (18.2% share); Pulsar +37% (~1.4 million units); 125cc+ segment ~50%+ of industry; KTM best-ever retail sales; Triumph Speed400/Scrambler400X launched; Chetak EV #1 milestone path (115,702 units FY2024).
- FY2025: Domestic two-wheelers ~4.03 million units total (2.03m motorcycles; -4.3% motorcycle share to 16.6% — weaker H2); Chetak #1 electric scooter (260,033 units); GOG started EV3W; exports 1.86 million units (+13.9%).
- FY2026: Total volumes 5.12 million units (+10%, crossing 5-lakh CVs); motorcycle segment share fell to 15.6%; KTM-Triumph >1.30 lakh units domestically; GST cut (28%→18% ≤350cc) Sep-2025 drove H2 festive rebound; record Pulsar year (>₹11,000 cr domestic; >₹16,500 cr global); new launches: KTM Duke 160, Adventure 390 R, Triumph Tracker 400, Thruxton 400, Pulsar refreshes; Chetak >7 lakh cumulative units.
- Sources: respective MD&A sections (charts/tables).

F2. Exports
- FY2024: 1.64 million units; £1,706m / ₹14,449 cr; turbulent macro, export declines.
- FY2025: 1.86 million units (+13.9%); ₹16,254 cr; LatAm still strong; EV/Region recovery.
- FY2026: 2.25 million units (+20.8%); ₹20,416 cr; >2 lakh/month Oct-2025; CV +49.2%; best since FY2022.
- Sources: respective ARs export tables.

F3. Commodity costs
- FY2026 MD&A: "margin pressures arising from an increasing commodity pressure"; steel/aluminium cost risk noted in Risks section. FY2024: low petroleum/aluminium benefited gross margins; FY2025: input cost press monitoring.

F4. New launches & EV
- FY2025: World's first CNG motorcycle (Freedom 125); GoGo EV3W; Chetak 35 series; new KTM Adventure/duke refresh; Triumph 400X/Scrambler.
- FY2026: Duke 160; Adventure 390 R; Thruxton 400; Tracker 400; Pulsar refreshes; WEGO + Riki EV; E100 flex-fuel; ADAS (cornering ABS etc.).
- EV investment: FY2025 EV revenue >₹5,500 cr (~20% of domestic sales); FY2026 EV revenue >₹8,000 cr (>20%); EV3W + E-rickshaw entry.
- Sources: respective ARs MD&A/R&D sections.

F5. Premiumisation / global portfolio
- FY2025: KTM-Triumph ~1 lakh domestic units; FY2026: >1.30 lakh (KT Showrooms, 80 outlets); global KTM-Triumph revenue ~₹5,000+ cr (+40% YoY) in FY2026.
